How to start a business: what you need to launch online

Starting a business today almost always includes a digital side: even an offline café or workshop gets clients through its website, maps and messaging apps. This checklist helps you miss nothing at launch.

Sikra · Updated 2026-09-18

Test the idea before spending big

Describe the client, their problem and what they are willing to pay. Build a simple page with the offer and an enquiry form and send a little paid traffic to it, or show it to people in your target audience. Enquiries and payments are the best proof of demand.

Registration and finance

  • Choose a legal form and register the company or sole proprietorship.
  • Open a business bank account with online banking.
  • Decide how you will issue invoices and receipts.
  • Keep personal and business money separate from day one.

The digital foundation of a business

  • A domain — short, without hyphens, registered to the company.
  • A website with a clear offer, starting prices, testimonials and a form.
  • Work email instead of a personal Gmail — clients trust an address on your domain more.
  • A CRM, or at least a single database of clients and enquiries.
  • Online payments with automatic receipts.
  • A Google Business Profile if you have a location or serve a specific city.
  • Analytics: Google Analytics 4 and Search Console.

Winning and keeping clients

Launch one or two channels and make them work: SEO and content, search ads, social media, partnerships. Every enquiry should land in the CRM. Collect consent for email and bring clients back with campaigns — the cheapest source of repeat sales.

Common mistakes when starting a business

  • Spending months on a “perfect” website instead of testing demand.
  • Putting the domain and accounts in a contractor’s name.
  • Ignoring unit economics: what a client costs and what they bring in.
  • Spreading across every channel at once.

Frequently asked questions

What do you need to start a business online?

A registered company and bank account, a domain, a website, work email, a CRM, payments and analytics — then one or two channels to win clients.

How much does a digital business launch cost?

It depends on scope: a simple website with email and a form costs far less than a shop with payments and integrations. We give you a scope and timeline after a short brief.

Can you start a business without a website?

You can start on social media or a marketplace, but a website on your own domain is an asset you own: it collects search traffic and does not depend on someone else’s platform rules.
